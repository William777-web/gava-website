FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions customers ask most — answers first.

What efficiency grades of filters do you offer?

Pre G1-G4, medium F5-F9, HEPA H13-H14 (≥0.3μm efficiency 99.97%~99.995%), ULPA U15-U17; also high-temperature 250-350°C, liquid-sealed, V-bank and PTFE-laminated options, matched to your conditions.

Are your filters individually tested?

Test reports can be provided subject to product and order confirmation; our engineers will confirm the exact details with you honestly.

Can I get samples first?

Yes. Samples or prototypes are sent for you to test before ordering; the sample policy (fees and freight) is stated clearly up front.

What is your lead time?

Lead time is confirmed per product, quantity and production schedule; we only promise what we can deliver.

Do you support custom / OEM production?

Yes. Filter bags, cloth, cartridges and mesh support made-to-order and OEM/ODM branding, plus bulk supply.

What if I don't know the exact specification?

Send us the equipment nameplate, old part or your operating conditions — we will recommend the right match. We handle the parameters for you.

Do you support export?

Export inquiries are welcome. Export documents, certification and logistics timing are confirmed per destination country and order; payment terms can be discussed.

Why choose GAVA TECH?

Evidence-based selection, calculable total cost, one-stop supply and samples first — we turn "comparing unit prices" into "calculating the total account".

Do you offer a warranty? What if there is a problem?

For specification mismatches or quality issues, send us photos and we will handle per agreement — replace or return as appropriate. After-sales support that actually answers.

Can you issue an invoice?

Yes. VAT invoices can be issued; rate and billing details per confirmation.

How does export cooperation work — payment, documents, logistics?

EXW / FOB / CIF supported; bulk orders T/T deposit + balance against B/L copy (commonly 30% + 70%), LC negotiable for larger projects — subject to mutual confirmation; VAT invoice, packing list and certificate of origin available, subject to order confirmation.

Quick Reference

Efficiency Grades (Air Filtration)

  • Pre G1-G4: ≥5μm, first-stage protection
  • Medium F5-F9: ≥1μm, second-stage
  • HEPA H13-H14: ≥0.3μm 99.97%~99.995%
  • ULPA U15-U17: ≥0.1μm 99.9995%+

Replacement Intervals (Guide)

  • Liquid filter bags: about 1-3 months
  • Dust bags: about 1-2 years (shorter in harsh conditions)
  • HEPA: about 6-12 months, per testing

Common Specifications

  • Liquid bags No.1-5, 1-300μm
  • Big-flow cartridge 40", membrane 8040 / 4040
  • High-temperature bags 204°C+, custom available

About GAVA TECH

GAVA TECH is GAVA's industrial-products supply brand, providing one-stop supply of filtration consumables, high-temperature protection and industrial components for factories and engineering customers, helping source filters, filter bags, filter cloth, cartridges, membranes and water-treatment consumables in one go.

We connect to domestic factory supply networks (mainly Zhejiang and Jiangsu) and serve both mainland-China industrial customers and export markets in Southeast Asia, the Middle East and North America; specific supply arrangements, documents and logistics are confirmed per inquiry.

Family-Factory Heritage

Rooted in Zhejiang, China since 2002, serving large customers in power, metallurgy and semiconductors with two decades of MRO and distribution experience — built on delivery, trust and reputation.
